## Why developers choose Firma

- **Minutes to integrate.** Simple REST API, OpenAPI specs, and pre-built UI components. Most teams ship in an evening.
- **Pay per envelope.** €0.049 (~5¢) per envelope. No subscriptions, no tiers, no surprises. Start for free.
- **Fully white-labeled.** Custom domains, branding, email templates, and certificates. Your users never see Firma.
- **Multi-tenant from day one.** Isolated workspaces for each of your customers with granular permissions.
- **Embeddable everything.** Drop-in template editor, signing request editor, and signing experience. Works with React, Vue, Angular, vanilla JS, and mobile.
- **Compliant and secure.** ESIGN, UETA, and eIDAS compliant. SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, GDPR. Audit trails and certificates of completion on every document.
- **Resell-ready.** Built to be rebranded and bundled. Turn e-signatures into a revenue stream.
